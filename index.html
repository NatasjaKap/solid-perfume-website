Our story

We wanted a perfume that fits in a coat pocket and never spills in a bag.

Alcohol-free

Beeswax, jojoba oil and shea butter carry the scent. It stays close to the skin and feels soft.

Compact

A small metal tin that fits any pocket. It is allowed in carry-on luggage.

Made for every day

Press a fingertip to the wax and touch your wrists, neck or behind your ears. Reapply when you like.

Stay in touch

News, new scents and the occasional offer. No more than one email a month.
